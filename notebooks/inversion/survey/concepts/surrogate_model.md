# Surrogate model

## Architecture and accuracy

- **v2 residual ensemble** at `~/surrogate_model/`. Load via:
  ```python
  from surrogate_model.surrogate import SurrogateModel
  model = SurrogateModel.load_default()
  ```
- **Inputs:** `(k1_body, k2_body, sp_angle_deg, ad_angle_deg, obs_dist_km)` — body-frame sun + observer unit vectors, plus solar-panel and antenna-dish articulation angles, plus observer distance. NOT `(q0, ω, t)`.
- **Output:** per-epoch predicted apparent magnitude.
- **Speedup:** ~50000× faster than the hi-fi BRDF + shadow ray-tracing pipeline.
- **Accuracy (training-time):** bright-MAE 0.008 mag (5.5× better than v1).
- **Survey API:** wrap via `lib.surrogate_eval.predict()`. Cached on first call.

## Bridge-independent — why the bug fix did NOT affect it

The surrogate maps body-frame coordinates directly to magnitude. There is no propagator in its training pipeline; the inputs were generated by the (unbuggy) renderer evaluating directly on body-frame inputs. The bug lived at the **bridge** from `(q0, ω, t)` to `(k1_body, k2_body)` — the propagator + renderer chain — not in the surrogate's body-frame → magnitude mapping.

This means: (a) the surrogate does NOT need retraining post-fix; (b) `surrogate.predict_magnitude(k1_body_truth, k2_body_truth, ...)` reproduces the cached `mag_hifi` from the truth NPZ to within the surrogate's intrinsic accuracy on every seed, regardless of buggy-vs-postfix; (c) the only place the bug is visible to the surrogate is in which `(k1_body, k2_body)` you choose to score — and at truth, the cached vectors in the post-fix NPZs are correct.

## Validated post-fix on seed 6

Smoke-test under correct truth, seed 6:
```
surrogate at truth k1/k2: pred range 6.52–16.40
cached mag_hifi:          range 6.52–16.43
full-LC MSE: 0.0029 mag²   →   ρ = 1.08  (Band A)
bright-MSE:  0.0036 mag²
```

The surrogate's intrinsic noise floor on seed 6 is ~Band A. This sets a per-seed floor on what surrogate-MSE-at-truth can be — the cost surface cannot drop below the surrogate's MAE squared at its argmin even if the argmin IS truth. Survey question 1 (cost-at-truth cohort scan) measures this distribution across all 100 seeds.

## The m145 caveat — landscape vs accuracy

m145 (frozen reference: `notebooks/inversion/wiki/wiki/experiments/m145_seed91_postfix_full_patch_chain.md`) found that on seed 91 under correct truth, the surrogate's **landscape** at fixed truth-ω has its global minimum at q0_err ≈ 135°, NOT at truth-q0. Surrogate accuracy at truth-q0 was unchanged by the bug fix (still ~0.003 MSE), but the surface around truth has a deceptive distant attractor that wins under DE search.

**This is one seed. Whether it generalises is open** — that is exactly what survey question 2 (surrogate landscape probe across PA-stratified seeds) exists to answer. Until then, do NOT assume:
- the surrogate is "wrong" everywhere
- the surrogate is "right" everywhere
- the deceptive q0=135° attractor is structural vs seed-specific

The survey should produce empirical answers, not extrapolate from one seed.

## What the survey may ultimately need

If question 2 finds the surrogate has deceptive global minima on a non-trivial fraction of seeds, options open up:
- surrogate-correction: residual learning on a small post-fix dataset
- surrogate ensembling at the landscape level (multiple seeds, ensemble disagreement as uncertainty signal)
- abandon surrogate-MSE as the primary cost surface and find an alternative

If question 2 finds the surrogate's argmin is at truth on most seeds, the failure mode is solver-side (DE escapes), and the path is a polish-from-input or tighter-bounded DE — not surrogate-side.
