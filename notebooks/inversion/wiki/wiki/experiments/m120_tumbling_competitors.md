# m120 — Tumbling-competitor test for surrogate-attitude-isoshell (multi-seed)

## Hypothesis

Truth (q0_true, ω_true) ranks in the top 0.1% of a ~10k TUMBLING-competitor pool under surrogate-residual cost variants. If true on the ATT_FAIL cohort (seeds 0, 27, 46, 58, 75 — the seeds where the classical pipeline fails because of attitude rather than omega error), the [[surrogate-attitude-isoshell]] branch survives the v2 weakness (static ω=0 grid, near-tautological) and becomes a valid discrimination tool across the seeds that actually need it.

Falsifiability: any seed has truth rank > 100 under ALL 13 variants → branch is seed-specific and doesn't generalise.

## Multi-seed headline (2026-04-15)

**Truth rank = 0 / 10004 under ALL 13 cost variants for ALL 6 seeds {0, 14, 27, 46, 58, 75}.** All `pass_top_0_1_pct`, `pass_top_1_pct`, and `pass_beats_wrong_any_variant` flags true per seed. Runtime ~5 min/seed on Pool(8), ~30 min total batch.

Seed 27's m115 basins have the largest ω error (3.08° dir, −0.53% mag) of the three seeds, and their ranks balloon to hundreds (mean_L1) or ~1800 (mean_L2). ## Method

Script: `notebooks/inversion/12_brightness_surface/m120_tumbling_competitors.py`. Reuses per-seed `setup.npz` (generated by m119v2 companion, 1-hr window matching m046, 500 constraint epochs).

### Pool construction (N = 10004)

| bucket | count | construction |
|--------|------:|--------------|
| known | 3–4 | truth + [[m115_surrogate_pipeline]] basins (seeds 0/14/27 have b0/b1/b2; seeds 46/58/75 have no m115 candidates → 3 entries with placeholders filled from perturbed truth for shape consistency) |
| uniform | 3000 | q0 ~ super-Fibonacci SO(3), ω direction ~ uniform S², \|ω\| ~ U[0.5·\|ω_true\|, 2·\|ω_true\|] |
| close_omega | 2000 | ω = ω_true rotated by N(0, 2°) + mag-scaled by 1 + N(0, 5%), q0 ~ uniform SO(3) |
| close_q0 | 2000 | q0 = q0_true ⊗ perturb(≤10°), ω uniform (as bucket 1) |
| near_truth | 3000 | (q0, ω) both perturbed at random scale ∈ {2°, 5°, 10°, 20°} |

### Scoring

For each candidate: propagate through 500 obs_times via `propagate_attitude` → `quats[500, 4]` → body-frame `k1_body, k2_body` → surrogate magnitude prediction → residual = pred − observed_mag_ce. 13 cost variants: mean_L2, mean_L1, max_abs, count_pass_{005, 010, 015, 020, 030, 050, 100}, soft_pass_{010, 020, 050}.

Parallelised via `multiprocessing.Pool(8)`. Throughput ~33 candidates/s steady state.

## What we learned (multi-seed)

### 1. The discrimination is NOT seed-specific

All 6 seeds land truth at rank 0 under all 13 variants. This was the refutation criterion for seed-specificity. The ATT_FAIL cohort (where classical pipelines fail for phi/attitude reasons) is where this matters most and the test passes there.

### 2. close_omega universally ranks ~8000

8004 for seeds 0/14/27 (bucket start 4004 in 10004-pool, bucket size 2000, so middle ≈ 5004 — but 8004 is actually the TOP of the bucket, meaning every close_omega candidate ranks worse than every uniform/close_q0/near_truth/known candidate). Same story at 8001 for seeds 46/58/75 (3 known candidates instead of 4 shifts bucket boundaries by 1).

Mechanism (sharpened 2026-04-15 via inline analysis of candidates.npz + residuals.npz across 4 seeds — see [[dark-mag-saturation]]):

- With ω=ω_true fixed, the body-frame sun direction traces the **same cone** as truth's (just rigidly rotated by the q0 offset). Truth's ω is by construction tuned so this cone sweeps bright facets (±X panels).
- A random q0 rotates the cone; for most rotations the rotated cone misses the bright lobes entirely.
- The surrogate therefore predicts **dark-side magnitudes** (~23 mag, its training-set ceiling) at essentially every epoch. Verified empirically: close_omega predictions are >20 mag for 92–100% of epochs across seeds 0, 14, 27, 46. Uniform candidates predict >20 mag in 0% of epochs (random ω decouples the cone from truth's bright-facet geometry).
- Residual = pred (~23) − obs (~13) ≈ +10 mag at every epoch → mean_L1 saturates near 10.
- Peak-count confirmation (seed 14): 100% of close_omega candidates have peak counts within 2 of truth's 114 peaks (same ω-magnitude preserves tumbling rate); only 3.3% of uniform candidates match. Peak-timing alone is not the mechanism — the saturation at the dark-mag ceiling is.

This is a property of the cost+pool+**surrogate**-ceiling combination. It sharpens the original "jointly sensitive to (q0, ω)" claim: the cost is effectively CAPPED in the close_omega region (flat at ~10), which is WORSE for gradient-based search (no gradient signal) but UNCHANGED for DE-style mutation-selection (good candidates still dominate the population).

See [[dark-mag-saturation]] for detailed evidence and implications for [[gradient-based-inversion]] initialisation.

### 3. Basin width: ≤ 20° in both q0 and ω

`near_truth` bucket best rank = 1 for all seeds. 3000 samples at {2°, 5°, 10°, 20°} scales — nothing beats truth. The cost landscape has a narrow deep basin confined well within 20° in both parameters. [[m121_basin_width_metric]] subsequently measured the basin directly and found it far tighter than 20°: q0 ≳ 5°, ω-direction < 0.1°, ω-magnitude < 0.25%. See [[basin-of-attraction]] for the surrogate-cost column (distinct from the classical alignment-cost basin).

### 4. m115 twin basins rank near truth only when ω is accurate

Seed 14 twin (ω_err 0.34°): ranks 17–83. Seed 0 twin (ω_err 2.9°): ranks 7–10 but the associated truth-adjacent basin b1 ranks similarly (9). Seed 27 twin (ω_err 3.08°): ranks 227–1861. The cost cannot distinguish the ±X twin from truth when ω is tight (known twin-degeneracy, not a branch failure). It CAN distinguish them when ω has any meaningful error.

### 5. Surrogate fidelity at truth consistent across seeds

Truth `mean_L1` scores: 0.054–0.057 mag across all 6 seeds (seed 0: 0.0557, seed 14: 0.0555, seed 27: 0.0539, seed 46: 0.0528, seed 58: 0.0570, seed 75: ~0.055). Tight cluster around σ_obs = 0.05 mag, confirming surrogate noise floor is consistent and the cost has no seed-dependent bias.

## What this does NOT establish

- **This is not a search algorithm test.** Truth was placed in the pool at index 0. The test shows the cost *ranks* truth correctly given truth is a candidate.
- **Pool hardness is asymmetric across buckets.** The uniform bucket's best rank 18–135 confirms 3000 uniform 6-DOF samples don't incidentally land near truth (volume fraction argument: 6-DOF basin ≈ 10⁻¹³, 3000 samples → prob 3×10⁻¹⁰ of accidental near-truth). This is adequate, but the test was always going to confirm that. The load-bearing discrimination work is done by the close_omega and close_q0 buckets (targeted adversaries).
- **Global search integration not tested.** Pair with [[surrogate-de-search]] or SO(3) × (ω grid) to find truth from scratch.
- **Bucket sampling is heuristic.** Doesn't prove the cost has NO sub-truth minima beyond 20° from truth — just that none of the 10k sampled candidates beat truth.

## Implication for [[surrogate-attitude-isoshell]] branch

Status upgrade: `#validated (single-seed)` → `#validated (multi-seed, ATT_FAIL cohort)`. The multi-seed gate specified in m119v2's decision tree is passed. Next phase is full integration with search (DE or isoshell intersection).

## Next steps

1. **Search integration.** Combine with [[surrogate-de-search]] — use DE to find point estimate, m120-style scoring to quantify basin of attraction.
2. **Tighter near-truth sampling.** Add {0.5°, 1°} scales to characterise basin curvature near truth.
3. **Broader population.** Test on 10-20 seeds beyond the ATT_FAIL cohort to make sure the discrimination survives when the cost landscape is benign (OK cohort) as well as when it's adversarial.
